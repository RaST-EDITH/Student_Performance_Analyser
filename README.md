# 📊 Student Performance Indicator

This project analyzes how various factors such as Gender, Ethnicity, Parental Level of Education, Lunch Type, and Test Preparation Course affect students' academic performance (test scores) in subjects like Math, Reading, and Writing. The analysis involves several stages, including data collection, exploratory data analysis, data preprocessing, and model training.

## 🔍 Problem Statement

This project aims to explore how various factors influence students' test scores. The key variables analyzed include:

- **Gender**
- **Race/Ethnicity**
- **Parental Level of Education**
- **Lunch Type**
- **Test Preparation Course**

---

## 🗃️ Data Collection

- **Dataset Source:** [Kaggle - Students Performance in Exams](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/spscientist/students-performance-in-exams?datasetId=74977)
- **Number of Columns:** 8
- **Number of Rows:** 1000

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### 🟢 Insights:

1. **Gender:** Female students generally outperform male students, except in Math.
2. **Race/Ethnicity:** Students from Groups A and B tend to perform worse than those from other groups.
3. **Parental Education:** Parental education level has minimal impact, except for male students whose parents hold Associate's or Master's degrees.
4. **Lunch Type:** Standard lunch correlates with better performance.
5. **Test Preparation Course:** Completing the test preparation course improves scores.

### 📊 Visualization Techniques:

- **Histograms & KDE Plots:** For average and total score distributions.
- **Bar Plots & Pie Charts:** To visualize categorical data like gender, race/ethnicity, and parental education.
- **Violin Plots:** To understand score distributions in Math, Reading, and Writing.
- **Box Plots:** To detect outliers in score distributions.
- **Pair Plots:** For multivariate analysis.

---

## 📈 Conclusions

- **Performance Influencers:** Lunch type, race/ethnicity, and parental education impact student performance.
- **Gender Gap:** Female students lead in overall performance but score lower in Math compared to male students.
- **Test Preparation:** Completing the preparation course is beneficial but not decisive.
- **Actionable Insight:** Focus on providing standard lunch and targeted support for students from Groups A and B to improve overall performance.
